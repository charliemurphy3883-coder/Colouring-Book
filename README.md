# My Colouring Book

A colouring-in app for little ones: Princesses, Unicorns, Cakes, Lollies, Animals, Puppy Pals, Things That Go and Fairytale (160 pictures each), plus a For Grown-Ups section with fine-line Mandalas, Patterns, Patterned Animals and Flower Gardens (160 each). 1,920 pictures in total.

Upload all 7 files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png, README.md

- Pick a category, then a picture.
- Tools: pencil (thin), pen, marker, paint brush (thick), eraser.
- Tap the big colour circle to open the colours: "Colours" (37 bright colours, or tap More colours for a 100-colour chart) and "Recent" (the last 24 colours used, newest first) or "Magic" (rainbow, glitter, fluoro, gold/silver, stars and hearts).
- Colour only goes inside the area where your finger first touched. Lift and touch another area to colour that one.
- Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on small areas and drag with two fingers to move around. The magnifying-glass button zooms back out.
- Undo arrow, bin (start again, asks first), green tick (saves and goes home).
- My Pages: tap the picture-with-a-plus tile to add your own colouring sheet from Photos or Files (a downloaded printable or a photo of a printed page). It's turned into clean outlines and colours just like the built-in pictures. Press and hold a page for about a second to delete it.
- Saved pictures show a star and appear in "My Pictures". Tap one to keep colouring it.

Pictures are saved on the iPad only. The version shows in the top-right corner of the home screen.
